Dear Nico Siapno,

Hi, asawa! I just want to greet you a happy birthday to you, my baby!!! 🩷✨

First of all, I want to thank you for being the strongest soldier in this relationship. Thank you for always being there for me, for inspiring me every time I wake up, go to school, and go home.

Thank you for not giving up on us, especially since the beginning of our relationship. From the time we met on Litmatch until now, you have remained one of the most important people in my life.

Thank you for listening to all of my stories, my jokes, my serious stories, my corny stories, my horny stories, and everything in between.

Thank you for being patient with my kakulitan, harot, kalibugan, and even when I call you “GURANG.” Thank you for accepting all of my personalities — the bubbly, harot, hyper, and everything else that comes with me.

I'm also sorry for the times that I became insensitive, for my shortcomings, and for the moments when I failed to understand you the way you deserved to be understood.

I know that our relationship isn't always easy. Especially because my parents still don't fully accept our relationship. But I still believe that someday, when they see how happy, successful, loving, and supportive we are together, they will understand us.
